# Deterministic experimentation and drift

Register policy revision, base version, prompt, cohort definitions, primary, guardrail, planned traffic, minimum useful effect, sample milestones and minimum duration before exposure. Keep client cohorts. Add disjoint language cohorts only where historical person counts and forecast live eligible traffic support both arms. Merge sparse strata into fallback or abstain. History informs planning and matching. Only actual epoch-attributed exposure enters the test.

Start with one candidate per cohort and one dimension at a time. Example allocation is 80% incumbent and 20% candidate, subject to exposure and policy. Persist planned weights and actual exposure. The simulator randomizes each dial. Stable assignment here means immutable plan and deterministic attribution to recorded treatments. Person-sticky randomized assignment needs provider support. Use first eligible dial for pickup and first eligible connected call for engagement, deduplicated across arms per KPI. Conditional engagement describes connected people and can change with pickup composition. Track business conclusions separately over uploaded enrollments.

Evaluate predeclared fixed prefixes, initially 200, 400, 800 and powers of two up to 51,200 per arm. Require at least two active calling periods for ordinary promotion, with a predeclared opportunity to collect enough connected observations. Test one-sided Fisher improvement and require an observed effect above the planned practical threshold, e.g. 2 pp. Report exact binomial-derived difference bounds, sample counts and p-values. Use separate one-sided Clopper–Pearson component bounds for the guardrail and require lower difference above minus the client margin. Preregister cohort count and candidate count, spend across epochs proportional to 1/e² and allocate each epoch's budget across cohorts, candidates and looks. Reserve 2% primary, 2% guardrail and 1% decay per workflow under stable independent sampling. For production clustered or time-varying exposure, validate on blocked randomized samples before extending claims. Outcome-dependent cohort creation or repeated tests outside the plan invalidate nominal rates.

At 25% pickup, a rough two-arm normal planning estimate for 2 pp lift with one-sided alpha .001 and 80% power is 2 × .25 × .75 × (3.09 + .84)² / .02² ≈ 14,480 dials per arm. With 20% treatment, forecast ~72,400 dials before that arm reaches the target. The separate 2 pp guardrail may require more calls because only connected calls count for engagement. Use exact calculations or simulation for final planning. Small cohorts will often abstain.

Promote only after both gates pass. Keep 20% on the original control alone. Pick non-overlapping fresh monitoring windows sized from sentinel traffic, with fixed looks and budget. Compare reversal on primary or material guardrail breach with appropriate one-sided bounds. Persist actionable rollback intent independently of subsequent windows. Require fresh configuration and approval, verify post-rollback exposure, and explicitly report drift that lacks adequate support. Use history and cohorts to diagnose caller health. A safe candidate prompt may come from the LLM, while deterministic ranking and governance decide whether to use it.

The evaluation controller now sets its monitoring duration at promotion from elapsed trial time and the smallest observed eligible cohort/KPI stream. It forecasts when 20% sentinel exposure could yield the first 200 observations, rounds to hours, and bounds the result between 3 and 24 hours. This is a forecast. Quiet periods and campaign locks can still leave a window inconclusive. Older trials retain the 3-hour default.
